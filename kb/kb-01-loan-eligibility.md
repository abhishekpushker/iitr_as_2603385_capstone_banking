Personal loan applicants must be aged 21–58, earn a net monthly income of at least ₹25,000 and
hold a credit score of 700 or more. Home loans need a credit score of at least 725, allow a
co-applicant, and must be fully repaid by the time the borrower turns 70. Auto loans need a
credit score of 680 or above and a net monthly income of at least ₹20,000. Education loans
require an admission letter from the institution, and a parent or guardian must join the loan
as co-borrower. Business loans need at least 3 years of business vintage, income tax returns
(ITR) for the last 2 years and an annual turnover of ₹40 lakh or more.
