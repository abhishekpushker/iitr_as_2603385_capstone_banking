Personal loan interest ranges from 10.5% to 24% p.a. by credit score band: 10.5–12% for scores
of 800 and above, 12–16% for 750–799 and 16–24% for 700–749. Home loans carry a floating rate
of 8.4–9.9% p.a. Auto loans are priced at 8.9–12.5%, education loans at 9.5–13% and business
loans at 14–22% p.a.
