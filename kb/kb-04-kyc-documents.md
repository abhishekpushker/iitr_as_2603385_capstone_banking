For KYC, a PAN is mandatory for every customer. You must also submit one address proof as a
KYC document, e.g. Aadhaar, a passport, a voter ID or a driving licence. Video KYC is accepted
in place of a branch visit. Periodic re-KYC is due every 2 years for high-risk customers, every
8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers.
