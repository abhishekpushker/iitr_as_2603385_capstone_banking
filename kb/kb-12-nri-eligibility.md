Non-resident Indians (NRIs) can open NRE and NRO accounts with a valid passport plus a valid
visa or OCI card. NRIs are eligible for home loans of up to 80% LTV of the property value, but
not for personal loans. EMIs must be repaid from an NRE or NRO account. An NRI may also be a
joint holder with a resident close relative on a former-or-survivor basis.
