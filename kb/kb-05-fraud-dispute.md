If you report an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days, you have zero liability for
it. If you report it within 4–7 working days, your liability is capped at ₹10,000. A
provisional credit for the disputed amount is made within 10 working days of your report, and
every dispute reaches a final resolution within 90 days.
