A joint account can have up to 4 holders. A joint account can be operated in one of three modes:
either-or-survivor, former-or-survivor or jointly. Removing a holder needs the written consent
of all holders. An NRI may be a joint holder with a resident close relative, but only on a
former-or-survivor basis.
