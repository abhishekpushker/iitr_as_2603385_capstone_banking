A Submitted application is picked up and reviewed within 2 working days. Under Review means the
credit team is assessing the file, which takes up to 7 working days. An application flagged for
fraud review can take up to 5 additional working days. An Approved loan moves to Disbursed
within 48 hours of approval, and a Rejected applicant may reapply after 90 days.
