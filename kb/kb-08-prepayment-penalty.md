Floating-rate home loans taken by individuals carry no prepayment penalty. Fixed-rate personal
loans charge a prepayment penalty of 4% of the prepaid principal during the first 12 EMIs and
2% after that. You may foreclose a loan in full only after 6 EMIs have been paid. Auto loans
allow free part-prepayment of up to 25% of the outstanding principal every year.
