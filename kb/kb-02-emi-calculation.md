Loan EMIs use the reducing-balance formula EMI = P·r·(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the
principal, n is the number of monthly instalments and r = annual rate/12/100. The first EMI
falls due on the 5th of the month after disbursal. Interest for the gap days between disbursal
and the start of the first EMI cycle is collected separately as broken-period interest. Every
EMI amount is rounded to the nearest rupee.
