topic: "Minimum balance"

disclaimer: "Fictional policy written for a capstone; not Cred's actual policy."
---
Savings accounts must keep an average monthly balance (AMB) of ₹10,000 at metro branches and
₹5,000 at semi-urban branches. A shortfall is charged at 6% of the shortfall amount, capped at
₹500 a month. Salary accounts and basic savings accounts are exempt from this requirement.
