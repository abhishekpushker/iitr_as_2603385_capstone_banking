Payment history weighs most in your credit score, so paying every EMI and card bill on time
matters more than anything else. Keep your credit utilisation below 30% of your total limit.
Each hard enquiry stays visible on your report for 24 months, and a healthy credit mix and a
longer average account age also help. A loan rejection does not lower your score by itself, but
the hard enquiry made for that application does.
