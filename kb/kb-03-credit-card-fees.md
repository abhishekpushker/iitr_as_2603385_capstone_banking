The credit card carries a joining fee of ₹500, which is waived if you spend ₹10,000 within 60
days of card issue. The annual fee of ₹999 is waived when your spend in the card year reaches
₹1.5 lakh. Purchases in a foreign currency attract a forex markup of 3.5%, and every cash
advance costs 2.5% of the amount withdrawn (min ₹500). Late payment fees follow a slab that
rises with the outstanding balance on the statement date.
