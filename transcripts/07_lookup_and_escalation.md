## Formula

```
recency          = min(max(days_since_created, 0), 30) / 30
escalation_score = round(0.4 · flagged + 0.6 · recency, 4)        ∈ [0, 1]
T                = round(0.6 · P80(days_since_created) / 30, 4)
escalate         ⇔ escalation_score ≥ T
```

P80 is the **nearest-rank** 80th percentile, `sorted(days)[ceil(0.8 · n) − 1]`, taken over the generated dataset (`dataset.LOAN_APPLICATIONS`) by `scripts/build_escalation_config.py`, which writes `config/escalation.json`. The lookup tool reads the weights and T back from that file.

## What T means

- **Unflagged** applications score `0.6 · days/30` and escalate from **day 25** — the P80 age. Those are the oldest applications in the queue: 14 of 60 (23.3%) are at least 25 days old (ties at 25 days push this above 20%). This is the stale-queue rule.
- **Flagged** applications start at 0.4 and escalate once `days ≥ 30 · (T − 0.4) / 0.6` = 30 × (0.5 − 0.4) / 0.6 = 5.0, i.e. from **day 5**, so a fraud flag is reviewed within days.
- It is a weighted blend, not a boolean OR: a brand-new flagged application scores 0.4 < T = 0.5 and does **not** auto-escalate.
- Known limitation: the score ignores status, so a flagged, already disbursed loan still escalates on age alone.
