**`kb-01-loan-eligibility`**

1. Personal loan applicants must be aged 21–58, earn a net monthly income of at least ₹25,000 and hold a credit score of 700 or more.
2. Home loans need a credit score of at least 725, allow a co-applicant, and must be fully repaid by the time the borrower turns 70.
3. Auto loans need a credit score of 680 or above and a net monthly income of at least ₹20,000.
4. Education loans require an admission letter from the institution, and a parent or guardian must join the loan as co-borrower.
5. Business loans need at least 3 years of business vintage, income tax returns (ITR) for the last 2 years and an annual turnover of ₹40 lakh or more.

**`kb-02-emi-calculation`**

1. Loan EMIs use the reducing-balance formula EMI = P·r·(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the principal, n is the number of monthly instalments and r = annual rate/12/100.
2. The first EMI falls due on the 5th of the month after disbursal.
3. Interest for the gap days between disbursal and the start of the first EMI cycle is collected separately as broken-period interest.
4. Every EMI amount is rounded to the nearest rupee.

**`kb-03-credit-card-fees`**

1. The credit card carries a joining fee of ₹500, which is waived if you spend ₹10,000 within 60 days of card issue.
2. The annual fee of ₹999 is waived when your spend in the card year reaches ₹1.5 lakh.
3. Purchases in a foreign currency attract a forex markup of 3.5%, and every cash advance costs 2.5% of the amount withdrawn (min ₹500).
4. Late payment fees follow a slab that rises with the outstanding balance on the statement date.

**`kb-04-kyc-documents`**

1. For KYC, a PAN is mandatory for every customer.
2. You must also submit one address proof as a KYC document, e.g. Aadhaar, a passport, a voter ID or a driving licence.
3. Video KYC is accepted in place of a branch visit.
4. Periodic re-KYC is due every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

**`kb-05-fraud-dispute`**

1. If you report an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days, you have zero liability for it.
2. If you report it within 4–7 working days, your liability is capped at ₹10,000.
3. A provisional credit for the disputed amount is made within 10 working days of your report, and every dispute reaches a final resolution within 90 days.

**`kb-06-account-closure`**

1. You can ask to close your account in the app or in writing.
2. All dues on the account must be cleared before the request is processed.
3. The account is closed within 7 working days, and the credit bureau is updated within 30 days of closure.
4. A closure fee of ₹500 applies if you close the account within the first year.

**`kb-07-interest-rate-slabs`**

1. Personal loan interest ranges from 10.5% to 24% p.a. by credit score band: 10.5–12% for scores of 800 and above, 12–16% for 750–799 and 16–24% for 700–749.
2. Home loans carry a floating rate of 8.4–9.9% p.a.
3. Auto loans are priced at 8.9–12.5%, education loans at 9.5–13% and business loans at 14–22% p.a.

**`kb-08-prepayment-penalty`**

1. Floating-rate home loans taken by individuals carry no prepayment penalty.
2. Fixed-rate personal loans charge a prepayment penalty of 4% of the prepaid principal during the first 12 EMIs and 2% after that.
3. You may foreclose a loan in full only after 6 EMIs have been paid.
4. Auto loans allow free part-prepayment of up to 25% of the outstanding principal every year.

**`kb-09-minimum-balance`**

1. Savings accounts must keep an average monthly balance (AMB) of ₹10,000 at metro branches and ₹5,000 at semi-urban branches.
2. A shortfall is charged at 6% of the shortfall amount, capped at ₹500 a month.
3. Salary accounts and basic savings accounts are exempt from this requirement.

**`kb-10-credit-score-factors`**

1. Payment history weighs most in your credit score, so paying every EMI and card bill on time matters more than anything else.
2. Keep your credit utilisation below 30% of your total limit.
3. Each hard enquiry stays visible on your report for 24 months, and a healthy credit mix and a longer average account age also help.
4. A loan rejection does not lower your score by itself, but the hard enquiry made for that application does.

**`kb-12-nri-eligibility`**

2. NRIs are eligible for home loans of up to 80% LTV of the property value, but not for personal loans.
3. EMIs must be repaid from an NRE or NRO account.
4. An NRI may also be a joint holder with a resident close relative on a former-or-survivor basis.
